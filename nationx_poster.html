📌 Project Motivation

Bangladesh's government services are scattered across disconnected websites and manual offices. Citizens waste hours navigating bureaucratic systems for basic needs like NID cards, passports, tax filing, and land records. We developed NATIONX — a single unified platform that digitizes 20+ government service modules, bringing everything under one login with real-time tracking, online payments, and transparent processing.

📋 Project Overview
NATIONX is a full-stack web application implementing a 3-Tier Client-Server Architecture that digitizes Bangladesh's central government services. It connects citizens to 20+ government departments through a single portal — from NID & Passport applications to Tax filing, Health services, Education results, Land management, Agriculture, Water resources, and more. The system features JWT authentication, role-based admin panels, SSLCommerz payment integration, and a MySQL database with 134+ normalized tables including stored procedures, triggers, and complex queries.
