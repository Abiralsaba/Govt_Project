📌 Project Motivation

Bangladesh's government services are scattered across disconnected websites and manual offices. Citizens waste hours navigating bureaucracy for NID cards, passports, tax filing, and land records. NATIONX is a single unified platform that digitizes 20+ government service modules — one login, real-time tracking, online payments, and transparent processing for all citizens.

📋 Project Overview
NATIONX is a full-stack web application implementing a 3-Tier Client-Server Architecture that digitizes Bangladesh's central government services. It connects citizens to 20+ government departments through a single portal — from NID & Passport applications to Tax filing, Health services, Education results, Land management, Agriculture, Water resources, and more. The system features JWT authentication, role-based admin panels, SSLCommerz payment integration, and a MySQL database with 134+ normalized tables (3NF & BCNF achieved) including stored procedures, triggers, views, and complex queries.
